The real receipt remains one grant. Native animation callbacks never call the
wallet writer. Replay/skip, coin taps, navigation, account invalidation and
lifetime changes cannot grant another reward. Examples such as 70→90 are
presentation of a confirmed +20, not multiple transactions.

Accessible wallet labels report the confirmed value, not every intermediate
number. Reel rows, plus accents and asset flourishes are decorative. The final
result is announced once. Production shows no replay or motion-control row. HTML playback controls live
in the separate Review panel; native reviewControls defaults false and is
available only in a labelled Playback QA preview. QA controls retain48dp targets.
The reason sits4dp below the event. CTA navigation can settle and leave immediately.

All finite effects stop or settle on offscreen/hidden state, reduced motion,
media error, route replacement or disposal. Reduced motion exposes the same
confirmed result and next actions without the movie, reel or turn. Sound
is opt-in during QA playback and defaults off. Production retains the app's
existing sound and reduced-motion policies; no replay control is added to the receipt.
Replay keeps the final amount and wallet; it never restarts the credit clock.
Initially hidden HTML receipts settle to their confirmed result as well.

Original acting RGB is preserved during alpha matting. No movie transform,
saturation, grayscale, spill-color correction or ancestor disabled filter is
used. Native lossless WebP compares all 121 visible RGB frames directly with
the original decoded MP4: max delta 0. VP9 has ordinary codec/YUV differences.

This is implementation and browser/widget evidence. Physical iPhone/Android
frame pacing, memory, audio synchronization, haptics and assistive technology
remain separate device QA.
