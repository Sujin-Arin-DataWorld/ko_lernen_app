# Brush mask asset

Built-in image_gen tool. Generated grayscale brush atlas, not a human-painted provenance claim. Palette stays in the existing native materials. Architecture is not generated.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one square grayscale brush-mark atlas for existing native Blender architectural materials. This is a technical texture, NOT a scene, NOT a building illustration.
Primary request: authentic considered editorial gouache brushwork with calm unpainted areas, authored broad shapes and sparse dry brush accents. It will replace over-dense procedural material grain. Show the human decision in the placement and varied ending of strokes.
Composition: exact 2 by 2 grid, each quadrant fills exactly one quarter with NO visible borders, labels, gutters or frames. No perspectives, shadows or light direction. Flat albedo-like masks only.
Top-left: timber brushwork, a handful of long vertical overlapping flat-brush strokes with gently interrupted edges, varied broad lengths, occasional fine bristle runs, calm broad midgray areas. NO photographic wood grain, NO knots, NO dense parallel pinstripes.
Top-right: clay roof tile brushwork, short gently curved repeated-but-varied brush passes and broad quiet areas, irregular endings; NO tile shapes, grout, roof or outlines.
Bottom-left: stone painter's marks, a few broad angular broken palette-brush patches of related middle grays with a little dry stipple at the ends; NO stones, pebbles, blocks, mosaic, gravel or dirt.
Bottom-right: plaster brushwork, extremely quiet broad overlapping rounded limewash brush passes, a few soft broken edges with subtle paper tooth.
All four panels are same coherent tasteful hand-painted illustration language with different mark rhythms. Neutral grayscale only. Midgray base about RGB 128, values broadly between 105 and 155, a few restrained accents up to 170/down to90. No pure black or white. Avoid adding global noisy grain. The detailed tooth should be sparse and subordinate. Seam-friendly edges: borders of each quadrant settle to same midgray. No text, no logos, no watermark, no decorative motifs.
