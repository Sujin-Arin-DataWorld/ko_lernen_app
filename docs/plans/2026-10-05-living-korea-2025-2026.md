# Living Korea 2025–26 — contemporary Korea persona-learning program

Date: 2026-10-05
Research window: 2025-10-05 through 2026-10-05
Branch: `session/living-korea-20251005-20261005-2026-10-05`

## Goal

Make contemporary Korea feel like the lived background of the eleven canonical
personas. The program does **not** turn the app into a current-events quiz.
Recent issues open believable situations; dialogue remains useful after the
headline ages.

Authoring pipeline:

1. source-backed current-Korea topic snapshot
2. persona fit and relationship check
3. real-life scene before pedagogy
4. natural dialogue in the persona's canonical voice
5. mine vocabulary, grammar, pragmatic expressions, and register markers
6. CEFR/LCP audit
7. source-backed contemporary culture card
8. freshness/archive review

TTS remains Jin-owned and is out of scope.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Dialogue comes before target grammar selection.
- Low CEFR means simpler language, not younger or less competent adults.
- Volatile numbers, rankings, and changing policy details belong in the culture
  card, not in evergreen dialogue unless the exact number is necessary.
- Legal, financial, medical, political, and security claims stay source-backed
  and within the speaking character's actual authority.
- Security scenes are defensive: account hygiene, permissions, verification,
  backups, updates, reporting, and safe escalation; no offensive how-to.
- Contemporary politics may provide civic context, but no partisan persuasion.
- Existing culture metadata remains derived/read-only and never becomes mastery,
  XP, or reward ownership.

Status: **complete**

Canonical audit:
`tools/content_factory/review/living_korea_lcp_audit_20261005.json`

The first-wave 9 scenes / 54 dialogue turns were re-audited through the
canonical `CefrLexicon + GrammarIndex` after D-3 mining.

Exit state:
- dialogue sentence unknown: **0**
- mined vocabulary unknown: **0**
- mined vocabulary over2: **0**
- maximum sentence level delta: **+1**
- no live scenario write
- no TTS write

Three different classes of debt were handled separately:

1. **True difficulty debt** was rewritten in the dialogue rather than hidden
   with level exceptions. B1 examples include replacing unnecessary
   production jargon such as `시안/촬영본/생성본/발신번호` with natural,
   task-equivalent language.
2. **Modern-life exact owners** were added narrowly for `링크` (A2),
   `폴더` (B1), and the user-reviewed digital-safety terms
   `보이스피싱/피싱` (B1), where the external lexicon either overgraded or
   did not own the actual contemporary-life word.
3. **Auditor morphology gaps** were repaired for normal surfaces including
   `어떨까요/누르래요/마세요/다르대/왔대/눌렀냐/할게/보내` plus the
   user-reviewed colloquial forms `보내서/빼가는/있다던데/조심해야겠어/
   들어가서/써서/알려줘야겠다/왔다는데/눌렀대지`.
4. Proper-name vocatives such as `수진아` and pure chat laughter such as
   `ㅎㅎ/ㅋㅋ` are now treated as non-lexical conversation support rather
   than unknown vocabulary.

These repairs lowered the global cloze sentence unknown count from
**282 to 277 / 14,721** while keeping the global ratchet at **1.89%**.
