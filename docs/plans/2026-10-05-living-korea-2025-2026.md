# Living Korea 2025–26 — contemporary Korea persona-learning program

Date: 2026-10-05
Research window: 2025-10-05 through 2026-10-05
Branch: `session/living-korea-20251005-20261005-2026-10-05`

## Goal

Make contemporary Korea feel like the lived background of the eleven canonical
personas. The program does **not** turn the app into a current-events quiz.
Recent issues open believable situations; dialogue remains useful after the
headline ages.

Authoring pipeline:

1. source-backed current-Korea topic snapshot
2. persona fit and relationship check
3. real-life scene before pedagogy
4. natural dialogue in the persona's canonical voice
5. mine vocabulary, grammar, pragmatic expressions, and register markers
6. CEFR/LCP audit
7. source-backed contemporary culture card
8. freshness/archive review

TTS remains Jin-owned and is out of scope.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Dialogue comes before target grammar selection.
- Low CEFR means simpler language, not younger or less competent adults.
- Volatile numbers, rankings, and changing policy details belong in the culture
  card, not in evergreen dialogue unless the exact number is necessary.
- Legal, financial, medical, political, and security claims stay source-backed
  and within the speaking character's actual authority.
- Security scenes are defensive: account hygiene, permissions, verification,
  backups, updates, reporting, and safe escalation; no offensive how-to.
- Contemporary politics may provide civic context, but no partisan persuasion.
- Existing culture metadata remains derived/read-only and never becomes mastery,
  XP, or reward ownership.

## Canonical persona-dialogue authoring contract — 2026-10-06

User review of both Living Korea waves exposed a repeatable quality rule: the
dialogue becomes substantially better when **relationship and personality carry
the information**, instead of characters taking turns delivering the topic.

Canonical contract:
`tools/content_factory/canonical_scenarios/dialogue_authoring_contract_20261006.json`

This contract now governs future new or materially revised persona-led Korean
dialogue across culture scenes, scenarios, listening dialogue, and
contemporary-Korea content.

Mandatory order:
1. canonical relationship / chronology / register
2. real-life task or friction
3. character-specific human beat
4. natural Korean dialogue
5. only then vocab / grammar / pragmatics / register mining
6. CEFR/LCP audit
7. if a human changes the Korean copy, discard stale mining and repeat mining
   + audit

A `human beat` is not a mandatory joke. It is one relationship-bearing beat
per ordinary six-turn scene where safe: teasing, dry humor, affection, a small
complaint, surprise, self-deprecation, a callback, or gentle disagreement.
Safety/fraud/health/legal/electrical scenes preserve relationship texture
without joking about the danger itself.

Register precedence is fail-closed:
- explicit relationship override
- relationship-specific profile rule
- private/work setting
- character base register
- CEFR simplification

Concrete locked examples:
- Sujin ↔ Christian: first meeting / early relationship = polite; current
  one-year dating timeline = mutual banmal
- Andrea ↔ Minho: private married-life dialogue = mutual banmal; workplace
  register never leaks into their home dialogue merely because work is the topic

The contract also records distinct human-beat styles for all eleven personas so
humor does not become interchangeable. A future authoring draft/brief must
reference the canonical contract path. `validate_content.py`,
`run_persona_culture_authoring.py`, and
`test_dialogue_authoring_contract.py` enforce that dependency.

This is intentionally a writing-quality contract, not a request to make every
scene comedic. The anti-pattern is **textbook exposition with character names
attached**.
