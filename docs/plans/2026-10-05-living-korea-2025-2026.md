# Living Korea 2025–26 — contemporary Korea persona-learning program

Date: 2026-10-05
Research window: 2025-10-05 through 2026-10-05
Branch: `session/living-korea-20251005-20261005-2026-10-05`

## Goal

Make contemporary Korea feel like the lived background of the eleven canonical
personas. The program does **not** turn the app into a current-events quiz.
Recent issues open believable situations; dialogue remains useful after the
headline ages.

Authoring pipeline:

1. source-backed current-Korea topic snapshot
2. persona fit and relationship check
3. real-life scene before pedagogy
4. natural dialogue in the persona's canonical voice
5. mine vocabulary, grammar, pragmatic expressions, and register markers
6. CEFR/LCP audit
7. source-backed contemporary culture card
8. freshness/archive review

TTS remains Jin-owned and is out of scope.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Dialogue comes before target grammar selection.
- Low CEFR means simpler language, not younger or less competent adults.
- Volatile numbers, rankings, and changing policy details belong in the culture
  card, not in evergreen dialogue unless the exact number is necessary.
- Legal, financial, medical, political, and security claims stay source-backed
  and within the speaking character's actual authority.
- Security scenes are defensive: account hygiene, permissions, verification,
  backups, updates, reporting, and safe escalation; no offensive how-to.
- Contemporary politics may provide civic context, but no partisan persuasion.
- Existing culture metadata remains derived/read-only and never becomes mastery,
  XP, or reward ownership.

## Stage D-2 — scene-first authoring

Status: **next**

Do not write eleven unrelated news scenes. Start with cross-persona arcs that
let the same vocabulary recur under different register and relationship
conditions.

Recommended first three arcs:

### D-2A — "이거 진짜 촬영본이에요?"

Characters: Maya → Daniel → Sujin → Christian
Topics: AI transparency + media provenance + basic system trust

Scene chain:
1. Maya receives a mixed real/AI campaign draft.
2. Daniel separates actual footage from generated inserts.
3. Sujin asks what the user needs to know in the final product.
4. Christian notices a system/asset provenance question but stays within his
   student-level systems/security knowledge.

Do not pre-force grammar. Candidate families after natural drafting:
`-는지`, `-아/어야 하다`, `-지 않게`, `-기는 하지만`.

### D-2B — "수업 중에는 안 돼"

Characters: Jun → Minho → Andrea → Christian
Topic: school smartphone rules

Scene chain:
1. Jun explains the new classroom rule in teenager language.
2. Minho asks what happens when a phone is actually needed.
3. Andrea separates house rules from school rules.
4. Christian compares his own student habits without becoming the authority.

Likely extractable language:
permission, prohibition, exception, reason, alternative.

### D-2C — "이 문자, 진짜 맞아요?"

Characters: Dongsun → Christian → Sujin → Byeongcheol
Topics: voice phishing + account verification

Scene chain:
1. Dongsun receives an urgent-looking message at the shop.
2. Christian says not to rush and checks only safe, basic signs.
3. Sujin verifies through an official channel rather than trusting the link.
4. Byeongcheol turns the result into a short family rule without lecturing.

Security boundary: verification and safe escalation only; never reproduce a
usable scam script or offensive technique.
