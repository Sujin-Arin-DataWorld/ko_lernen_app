# Batch 39 — existing-glossary persona culture expansion

> Status: review-only authoring design. No live promotion, mastery, reward, or TTS change.

## Goal

Expand the promoted persona-culture world using **existing persona relationships and existing CulturalGlossary entries first**.

Priorities:
1. increase Minho's practical scene presence without turning him into a heritage explainer;
2. reuse glossary entries that are live but not yet linked to scenarios;
3. keep culture inside a concrete real-life task;
4. write natural dialogue before vocabulary leveling;
5. reuse the Batch 38 authoring pipeline unchanged.

## Persona boundaries

### Andrea × Minho
- family decision, not an etiquette lecture;
- Minho may describe what their family does, not a universal Korean rule;
- bojagi remains a reusable wrapping object, not a reward mechanic.

### Minho × Christian
- Minho is an IT mentor, not a hiring authority;
- the hanok café is a real meeting location, not a museum lesson;
- Christian asks functional questions about the space, not generic "Korea is amazing" reactions.

### Lena × Maya
- Lena focuses on use, gift context, preference, and appropriateness;
- Maya is not a traditional-craft expert;
- buchae is selected as a practical gift, not treated as costume.

### Maya × Daniel
- a short promotional edit is explicitly an excerpt;
- no claim that one clip represents all pansori;
- credit/source context stays visible.

### Hyuna × Daniel
- Hyuna protects context and local-participant boundaries;
- Daniel owns filming execution;
- filming permission and publication visibility remain separate decisions.
