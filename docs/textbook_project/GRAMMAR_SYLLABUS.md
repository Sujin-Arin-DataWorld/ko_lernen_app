# GRAMMAR SYLLABUS — research seed

Purpose: map grammar to communicative function, prerequisite knowledge, native usage, learner difficulty and EN/DE-specific explanation. Existing app grammar is a seed; final placement must be audited against the Korean Standard Curriculum and learner evidence.

## Required record for every grammar point
grammar_id; canonical_form; level/book/unit; function; form/allomorphy; constraints; spoken_variants; written_variants; register; prerequisite_ids; contrast_ids; native_examples; EN_explanation; DE_explanation; EN_error_prediction; DE_error_prediction; assessment_targets; source_ids; review_status.

## Level 1 core families
- Copula/existence: 이에요/예요, 있어요/없어요.
- Core particles: 은/는, 이/가, 을/를, 에, 에서, 도, 만, 하고/이랑.
- Polite present/past and basic future/plan: -아요/어요, -았/었어요, -(으)ㄹ 거예요.
- Negation: 안, 못; basic distinction before introducing -지 않다.
- Requests/invitations: 주세요, -아/어 주세요, -(으)세요, -(으)ㄹ까요?, 같이.
- Linking/reason basics: -고, -지만, -아/어서.
- Questions, counters, demonstratives, basic attributive forms only as needed by communicative tasks.

## Level 2 core families
- Experience: -아/어 보다, -(으)ㄴ 적이 있다.
- Ability/possibility: -(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다.
- Obligation/prohibition: -아/어야 하다, -(으)면 안 되다.
- Intention/promise: -(으)려고 하다, -(으)ㄹ게요.
- Conditions/comparison: -(으)면, 보다, -(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다.
- Sequence/simultaneity: -기 전에, -(으)ㄴ 후에, -(으)면서.
- Relative clauses and time since: -는/-(으)ㄴ/-(으)ㄹ N, -(으)ㄴ 지.
- Indirect quotation introduced gradually by speech-act type.

## Level 3 core families
- Background/discovery: -는데, -더라고요, -던데요.
- Change/result: -게 되다, -아/어지다.
- Purpose/attempt: -(으)려고, -기 위해(서), -아/어 보니.
- Reported speech system and contracted spoken forms.
- Guess/evidence/stance: -나 보다, -(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다, -(으)ㄹ 것 같다.
- Contrast/concession: -기는 하지만, -아/어도, -(으)ㄴ/는 반면.
- Nominalization and practical written forms: -기, -(으)ㅁ.

## Level 4 core families
- Cause/stance with nuance: -느라고, -는 바람에, -는 탓에, -길래.
- Counterfactual/hypothetical: -았/었더라면, -(으)ㄹ 텐데.
- Emphasis/limitation: -기 마련이다, -는 셈이다, -뿐만 아니라.
- Discourse connection: -는 데다가, -더라도, -(으)ㄴ/는 만큼.
- Professional written/official constructions and controlled 합니다체 expansion.

## Levels 5–6
Organize by discourse function rather than isolated endings: evidentiality, epistemic stance, evaluation, concession, causality, rhetorical contrast, nominalized written style, quotation/attribution, impersonal/public style, genre-specific cohesion, argumentation and stylistic transformation.

## EN learner contrast priorities
Particles/topic-subject distinction; subject omission; SOV and modifier order; honorifics/speech levels; counters; verb/adjective category differences; tense-aspect vs English tense; articles/plural not mapping directly.
Learner-corpus studies show particle omission/replacement remains a major English-L1 difficulty, including at higher proficiency, so particles require spiral recycling rather than one early chapter.

## DE learner contrast priorities
Do not explain particles as if they were simply German cases. Contrast Korean topic/subject/object marking with German case/article marking explicitly.
Use German clause architecture as a bridge only when useful: Korean verb-final order can be compared with German subordinate-clause verb-final patterns, while warning that the systems are not equivalent.
Track Sie/du expectations separately from Korean speech levels; politeness is relational/register-based, not a one-to-one pronoun substitution.

## Placement gate
A grammar point is not assigned to a level because it is “simple-looking.” Placement depends on communicative necessity, form complexity, semantic/pragmatic load, corpus frequency, prerequisites and observed learner errors.
