# Korean 1A · Unit 03
# Finding something in a shop

## By the end of this unit, you can:

- Ask where an item is in a shop and understand a simple location answer.
- Begin to distinguish 은/는 and 이/가 through real question-answer contexts.

## Why this unit exists

Use particles to find and identify objects instead of memorizing abstract particle definitions.

## 1. Core scene

**Learner**
저기요. 두부는 어디에 있어요?
*Excuse me, where is the tofu?*

**Staff**
저쪽 냉장고 옆에 있어요.
*Over there, next to the refrigerated section.*

**Learner**
이거 두부예요?
*Is this tofu?*

**Staff**
아니요, 그건 치즈예요.
*No, that's cheese.*

**Learner**
그럼 이거예요?
*Then is it this one?*

**Staff**
네, 그게 두부예요.
*Yes, that's tofu.*

### First task

Do not translate every word. Identify:
- who is speaking;
- what the immediate goal is;
- which line solves the problem.

## 2. What you should be able to say

- **___는 어디에 있어요?**
- **___에 있어요**
- **이거 ___예요?**
- **아니요, 그건 ___예요**
- **그럼 이거예요?**

These are the productive chunks for this unit.

### Productive grammar

- **은/는** — established topic / contrast
- **이/가** — subject/focus in identification/existence
- **에 있다** — location/existence

The goal is not to memorize labels. Use the forms to solve the scene.

## 3. Understand first — do not overlearn yet

- No extra recognition-only form in this unit.

Authentic input can contain grammar above your current production target. Understanding it does not mean you must produce it now.

## 4. Living Korean

### Pragmatics

- 저기요 can be useful for getting staff attention; tone matters.
- Correcting a wrong item can be brief and neutral.

### Culture/context

- Service attention strategies vary; avoid teaching one form as the only correct way.

Avoid the rule “Koreans always say X.” Learn who says what, to whom, and why.

## 5. English-speaker transfer notes

### EN-A1-PARTICLES-OMIT-REPLACE

**Risk:** Particle omission or replacement remains a major error class for English-speaking Korean learners.

**Teaching response:** Never teach particles once-and-done. Spiral them through noun+predicate frames and information context.

### EN-A1-TOPIC-SUBJECT

**Risk:** Treating both as a single English-style subject marker or swapping them without discourse motivation.

**Teaching response:** Teach mini-context pairs: '저는...' for frame/topic vs '누가? 제가...' for focused/new subject. Delay exhaustive theory.

### EN-A1-E-ESEO

**Risk:** Using one generic location marker because English 'at/in' does not align with Korean predicate-conditioned location marking.

**Teaching response:** Teach scene contrast: 학교에 가요 / 학교에 있어요 / 학교에서 공부해요 with movement/existence/action rather than translation labels.

## 6. Sound focus

- 은/는 and 이/가 attachment
- 있어요 linking
- 이거/그거 deictic rhythm

## 7. Input mission

- Choose the correct item/location from a shop dialogue.
- Notice established topic vs newly focused item.

## 8. Output mission

- Ask for two items in a simulated store.
- Correct one wrong identification.

## 9. Reading

- Read simple shelf labels.

## 10. Writing

- Complete short location notes with particles.

## 11. Interaction mission

Use an information-gap store map: your partner knows where the items are.

## 12. Exit task

You must be able to produce:

- ___는 어디에 있어요?
- ___에 있어요

You must be able to recognize:

- 은/는
- 이/가

**Success:** Use particles in a meaningful context rather than by translation alone.

## Production ceiling

Keep topic/subject theory minimal; context comes first.
