# Korean 1A · Unit 08
# Repairing a conversation when you miss something

## By the end of this unit, you can:

- Choose a repair strategy depending on whether you did not hear, did not know the meaning, or want confirmation.
- Check important information such as numbers, names, and exits.

## Why this unit exists

Use short repair strategies to keep the conversation going instead of pretending to understand.

## 1. Core scene

**Learner**
시청은 몇 번 출구예요?
*Which exit is for City Hall?*

**Staff**
13번 출구예요.
*Exit 13.*

**Learner**
3번 출구요?
*Exit 3?*

**Staff**
아니요, 13번이요.
*No, exit 13.*

**Learner**
다시 말씀해 주세요.
*Could you say that again?*

**Staff**
십삼 번 출구예요.
*Exit thirteen.*

### First task

Do not translate every word. Identify:
- who is speaking;
- what the immediate goal is;
- which line solves the problem.

## 2. What you should be able to say

- **다시 말씀해 주세요**
- **한 번만 더 말씀해 주세요**
- **천천히 말씀해 주세요**
- **잘 못 들었어요**
- **무슨 뜻이에요?**
- **제가 잘 이해한 게 맞아요?**
- **13번이요?**

These are the productive chunks for this unit.

### Productive grammar

- **-아/어 주세요** — repeat/slow request
- **무슨 N** — meaning question, chunk-first
- **아니에요** — correct information

The goal is not to memorize labels. Use the forms to solve the scene.

## 3. Understand first — do not overlearn yet

- **-는데요** — natural interpersonal softening but above productive target for this unit
- **계신지** — honorific embedded form; recognition/context only
- **몰랐어요** — past explanation; not target grammar here
- **-ㄹ게요** — promise/next action; recognition only
- **뭐라고요?** — intonation-sensitive and can sound surprised/challenging; recognition first

Authentic input can contain grammar above your current production target. Understanding it does not mean you must produce it now.

## 4. Living Korean

### Pragmatics

- Not hearing, not understanding a word, and confirming meaning are different repair problems.
- Very short repair forms are strongly intonation-sensitive.
- Repair is a learner-autonomy strategy.

### Culture/context

- Politeness in repair depends on relationship and tone; no single English translation is safe in every situation.

Avoid the rule “Koreans always say X.” Learn who says what, to whom, and why.

## 5. English-speaker transfer notes

### EN-A1-SPEECHLEVEL

**Risk:** Expecting politeness to be optional lexical courtesy rather than sentence-final grammar.

**Teaching response:** Treat sentence ending as relationship information. Keep 해요체 productive default; introduce register switching as recognition/social event.

## 6. Sound focus

- 3 vs 13
- question echo intonation
- 말씀해 주세요 / 천천히 phrase rhythm

## 7. Input mission

- Classify hearing vs meaning vs confirmation.
- Hear 3 vs 13 and repair the misunderstanding.

## 8. Output mission

- Perform all three repair functions.
- Confirm one name or number.

## 9. Reading

- Read a short learner-staff exchange and choose the repair type.

## 10. Writing

- Write one polite chat repair message.

## 11. Interaction mission

Your partner intentionally mumbles once, uses one unknown word, and gives one ambiguous number. Repair each problem.

## 12. Exit task

You must be able to produce:

- 다시 말씀해 주세요
- 무슨 뜻이에요?
- 13번이요?

You must be able to recognize:

- 뭐라고요?
- 뭐라고 했어?

**Success:** Use the right repair type without switching languages.

## Production ceiling

Repair strategies only; advanced grammar from supplementary scenes stays recognition-only.

This unit is part of a spaced-recycling curriculum. The target language returns in later contexts.
