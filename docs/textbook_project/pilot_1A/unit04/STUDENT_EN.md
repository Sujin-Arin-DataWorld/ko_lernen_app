# Korean 1A · Unit 04
# Ordering food and quantities

## By the end of this unit

In a Korean snack bar or café, you can:

- order a food or drink;
- add a simple quantity;
- ask one short question such as “Is it very spicy?”;
- change one condition, such as mild / iced / to go;
- understand a few common staff-side service phrases.

You do **not** need to reproduce every sentence the staff member uses.

---

## 1. Listen first

### Scene: at a bunsik restaurant

**Staff**  
주문하시겠어요?

**Customer**  
떡볶이 하나 주세요.

**Customer**  
많이 매워요?

**Staff**  
조금 매워요. 안 매운 것도 있어요.

**Customer**  
그럼 안 매운 걸로 주세요.

**Staff**  
네, 안 매운 걸로 드릴게요.

### Check the meaning

1. What does the customer order?
2. Do they want the spicy or mild version?
3. Which sentences are useful for the learner to say? Which ones are mainly useful to understand?

### Your production target

**N 하나 주세요.**  
One N, please.

**많이 매워요?**  
Is it very spicy?

**안 매운 걸로 주세요.**  
The mild one, please.

## 2. A powerful ordering frame

### N 주세요

**물 주세요.**  
Water, please.

**메뉴 주세요.**  
A menu, please.

In real orders, you often add a quantity.

### N 하나 주세요

**떡볶이 하나 주세요.**  
One tteokbokki, please.

**샌드위치 하나 주세요.**  
One sandwich, please.

### N 한 잔 주세요

**커피 한 잔 주세요.**  
One coffee, please.

**차 한 잔 주세요.**  
One tea, please.

### Important

Korean often uses a counting unit.

For now, do **not** memorize a giant counter chart.

Learn these as useful chunks:

- 하나
- 한 잔

You will expand the system later.

## 3. 을/를 when describing what you eat or drink

Compare:

**고기를 먹어요.**  
I eat meat.

**채소를 먹어요.**  
I eat vegetables.

**커피를 마셔요.**  
I drink coffee.

The object is often marked with **을/를**.

- after a consonant → **을**
  - 밥을
  - 물을
- after a vowel → **를**
  - 커피를
  - 고기를

But in a compact service order:

**떡볶이 하나 주세요.**

the noun can appear naturally without 을/를.

### Why?

These two sentences do different jobs:

**저는 떡볶이를 먹어요.**  
You are describing what you eat.

**떡볶이 하나 주세요.**  
You are ordering right now.

So do not learn either of these bad rules:

> “Particles are optional.”

or

> “Every noun must always have a particle.”

Learn the **scene + sentence function**.

## 4. Change one condition

### Spiciness

**많이 매워요?**  
Is it very spicy?

**조금 매워요.**  
It is a little spicy.

**안 매운 것도 있어요.**  
There is a mild version too.

**안 매운 걸로 주세요.**  
The mild one, please.

### Takeaway

**포장해 주세요.**  
To go, please. / Please pack it to go.

The staff may ask:

**여기서 드시고 가세요, 아니면 포장이세요?**

You do not need to analyze the entire sentence yet.

You only need to understand the choice:

> for here or to go?

and be able to answer:

**포장해 주세요.**

# Living Korean
## Safe learner Korean and real service Korean

A safe, natural default:

**아메리카노 한 잔 주세요.**

A compact order you may hear or use:

**이거 하나 주세요.**

Staff-side language you should recognize:

**뭐 드릴까요?**  
What can I get you?

**따뜻한 걸로 드릴까요, 아이스로 드릴까요?**  
Would you like it hot or iced?

**포장이세요?**  
To go?

### Optional contemporary shorthand

You may see **아아** used for 아이스 아메리카노 in some contexts.

It is useful recognition material, but it is **not required beginner production**.

> A word being used by some Koreans does not automatically make it a core A1 target.

---

## English-speaker trap: 주세요 is not “Give me...”

There is no single fixed English translation for 주세요.

Natural service equivalents include:

- **Can I get an Americano, please?**
- **Can I have an Americano, please?**
- **I'll have an Americano, please.**

Do not memorize:

> 주세요 = Give me...

“Give me...” is possible English, but it can sound too command-like as a default service formula.

### Counter trap

English may simply say:

> two coffees

Korean often asks you to notice **what kind of thing is being counted**.

For drinks:

**커피 한 잔**  
one cup/glass of coffee

Start with chunks. Do not try to master every counter at once.

## 5. Sound and rhythm

### 주세요

Say it as one smooth phrase.

**주세요**

### 한 잔 주세요

Group it as:

**한 잔 | 주세요**

### 떡볶이 하나 주세요

Group it as:

**떡볶이 | 하나 | 주세요**

Your goal is clear, comfortable speech.

Speed is not the goal.

**떡볶이 하나 주세요.**

## 8. Speaking mission

Work with a partner and switch roles.

### Customer card

Include:

1. one menu item;
2. one quantity;
3. one condition.

Possible conditions:

- mild
- iced
- to go

### Staff card

Use two:

- 뭐 드릴까요?
- 많이 매워요. 괜찮으세요?
- 아이스로 드릴까요?
- 포장이세요?

The staff phrases are mainly **interaction input**.

You do not need to memorize all of them as productive grammar.

## 9. Exit task

Close the explanation pages.

Look at a menu and order without translating first.

Success means you can:

- name the item;
- use 하나 or 한 잔;
- use 주세요;
- ask or change one condition;
- respond to one short staff question.

Possible answers:

**아메리카노 한 잔 주세요. 아이스로 주세요.**

**떡볶이 하나 주세요. 많이 매워요?**
