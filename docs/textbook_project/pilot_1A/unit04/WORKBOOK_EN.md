## E. Staff language — meaning first

### 1

**뭐 드릴까요?**

A. What would you like to order?  
B. Where are you going?  
C. What time is it?

### 2

**포장이세요?**

A. Are you eating here?  
B. Is it to go?  
C. Is it spicy?

### 3

**안 매운 것도 있어요.**

A. There is a mild option too.  
B. There is no food.  
C. You cannot order now.
