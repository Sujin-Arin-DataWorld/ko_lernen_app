# 이름과 출신 말하기

Status: DRAFT_MATERIALIZED

## Can-do

- 이름과 출신을 말하고 상대의 이름·출신을 간단히 물을 수 있다.
- 한 번 말한 주어를 매 문장 반복하지 않고 짧은 자기소개 대화를 이어 갈 수 있다.

## Communicative problem

처음 만난 사람과 자기소개를 독백처럼 외우는 대신 짧게 말하고 되묻는다.

## Relationship / register

- Primary relationship: new peers / classmates
- Korean register: polite
- EN default: first-name neutral
- DE default: contextual du/Sie

## Productive language

- 은/는: set personal topic
- 이에요/예요: identify self
- N에서 왔어요: origin/source

## Formulaic production

- none

These are learner-usable chunks whose full grammar analysis is intentionally deferred.

## Recognition/context only

- -(으)셨어요: authentic polite question form may appear; productive honorific morphology is not analyzed exhaustively here

## Teaching order

1. play/read the scene;
2. establish meaning;
3. notice core chunks;
4. explain only necessary form;
5. contrast native-use variation;
6. address EN/DE transfer risk;
7. run interaction task;
8. delayed retrieval.

## EN-L1 risk

- **EN-A1-TOPIC-SUBJECT**: Treating both as a single English-style subject marker or swapping them without discourse motivation.
- **EN-A1-SUBJECT-OMISSION**: Repeating 저는/제가 or explicit pronouns because English clauses normally require overt subjects.
- **EN-A1-WORDORDER**: Transferring English SVO sequencing into Korean production.

## DE-L1 risk

- **DE-A1-TOPIC-FOCUS**: Searching for a German case-form equivalent instead of learning Korean information structure.
- **DE-A1-PARTICLE-CASE**: Treating 은/는, 이/가, 을/를 as equivalents of German Nominativ/Akkusativ or articles.
- **DE-A1-SUBJECT-OMISSION**: Restoring ich/du mechanically in Korean because German finite clauses usually express a subject.
- **DE-A1-WORDORDER**: Overusing German clause analogies ('Korean is like a German subordinate clause') until learners misgeneralize.

## Pragmatics

- one disclosure + one follow-up beats question barrage
- job/age/residence are optional follow-ups, not mandatory identity fields

## Culture caution

- Korean first-meeting questions can reflect relationship positioning; avoid presenting age/job as universally required.

Do not convert contextual patterns into universal rules.

## Pronunciation

- 이에요/예요 linking
- country/name syllable blocks
- 왔어요 pronunciation awareness

## Assessment

Must produce:
- 저는 ___이에요/예요
- ___에서 왔어요

Must recognize:
- 어디에서 오셨어요?

Success:
exchanges identity information without memorized paragraph
