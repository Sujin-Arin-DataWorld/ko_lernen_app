# Teacher Guide — Korean 1A Unit 01
# 첫 인사와 다시 묻기

Status: DRAFT_MATERIALIZED

## Can-do

- 처음 만난 사람에게 짧게 인사하고 네/아니요로 반응할 수 있다.
- 못 들었을 때 정중하게 한 번 더 말해 달라고 할 수 있다.

## Communicative problem

처음 한국에 도착하거나 처음 만난 상대의 말을 다 못 알아들어도 대화를 끊지 않고 인사와 repair를 이어 간다.

## Relationship / register

- Primary relationship: traveler ↔ public-service official / new acquaintance
- Korean register: polite
- EN default: neutral polite
- DE default: Sie in service; contextual elsewhere

## Productive language

- -아/어 주세요: polite request

## Formulaic production

- 감사합니다: 감사합니다 — high-frequency fixed thanks formula; does not imply productive control of 합니다체

These are learner-usable chunks whose full grammar analysis is intentionally deferred.

## Recognition/context only

- 이에요/예요: appears naturally in the airport response; active copula work begins in Unit 02
- -(으)시-: subject honorification appears in authentic service speech; do not teach as generic extra-politeness marker
- 합니다체 어미 전반: full formal-style morphology is deferred; fixed greetings may still be used as chunks

## Teaching order

1. play/read the scene;
2. establish meaning;
3. notice core chunks;
4. explain only necessary form;
5. contrast native-use variation;
6. address EN/DE transfer risk;
7. run interaction task;
8. delayed retrieval.

## EN-L1 risk

- **EN-A1-SPEECHLEVEL**: Expecting politeness to be optional lexical courtesy rather than sentence-final grammar.
- **EN-A1-HONORIFIC-SI**: Interpreting subject honorification as a general extra-politeness marker.

## DE-L1 risk

- **DE-A1-DU-SIE**: Assuming 존댓말=Sie and 반말=du.
- **DE-A1-HONORIFIC-SUBJECT**: Looking for a German pronoun/register equivalent for Korean subject honorification.

## Pragmatics

- service politeness
- repair is normal, not failure
- short response is acceptable

## Culture caution

- Korean politeness is carried heavily by sentence endings; it is not equivalent to English sir/ma'am or German Sie alone.

Do not convert contextual patterns into universal rules.

## Pronunciation

- 안녕하세요 rhythm
- 네? vs 네. intonation
- 말씀해 주세요 phrase rhythm

## Assessment

Must produce:
- 안녕하세요
- 다시 말씀해 주세요
- 감사합니다

Must recognize:
- 네?
- 한국은 처음이세요?

Success:
maintains interaction after one comprehension breakdown

## Production ceiling

short single-clause A1 turns; no explanation of full honorific system
