# Korean 1A · Unit 06
# Getting around and saying where to stop

## By the end of this unit, you can:

- Ask and confirm a destination, stop, or exit in transport situations.
- Begin to distinguish basic spatial uses of 에/에서/(으)로 through scenes rather than translation tables.

## Why this unit exists

Ask for a route or stop, then repair the conversation if the direction is wrong.

## 1. Core scene

**Driver**
이 근처 맞으시죠?
*It's around here, right?*

**Learner**
네. 여기 세워 주세요.
*Yes. Please stop here.*

**Driver**
정문 앞은 차가 많아요.
*There's a lot of traffic by the main entrance.*

**Learner**
그럼 저기 앞에 세워 주세요.
*Then please stop up there.*

**Driver**
네, 알겠습니다.
*Sure.*

**Learner**
감사합니다.
*Thank you.*

### First task

Do not translate every word. Identify:
- who is speaking;
- what the immediate goal is;
- which line solves the problem.

## 2. What you should be able to say

- **여기 세워 주세요**
- **몇 번 출구예요?**
- **어디서 갈아타요?**
- **이 버스 ___ 가요?**
- **저기 앞에 세워 주세요**

These are the productive chunks for this unit.

### Productive grammar

- **에** — destination/location
- **에서** — action/source contrast, limited use
- **(으)로** — direction/means, chunk-first
- **-아/어 주세요** — request stop/direction

The goal is not to memorize labels. Use the forms to solve the scene.

## 3. Understand first — do not overlearn yet

- **맞으시죠?** — driver service-side confirmation; learner understands without productive analysis

Authentic input can contain grammar above your current production target. Understanding it does not mean you must produce it now.

## 4. Living Korean

### Pragmatics

- Short route questions are natural.
- App/UI wording and human speech are not the same register.

### Culture/context

- Transit shorthand can be city-specific; keep it in local culture capsules.

Avoid the rule “Koreans always say X.” Learn who says what, to whom, and why.

## 5. English-speaker transfer notes

### EN-A1-E-ESEO

**Risk:** Using one generic location marker because English 'at/in' does not align with Korean predicate-conditioned location marking.

**Teaching response:** Teach scene contrast: 학교에 가요 / 학교에 있어요 / 학교에서 공부해요 with movement/existence/action rather than translation labels.

### EN-A1-PARTICLES-OMIT-REPLACE

**Risk:** Particle omission or replacement remains a major error class for English-speaking Korean learners.

**Teaching response:** Never teach particles once-and-done. Spiral them through noun+predicate frames and information context.

## 6. Sound focus

- 출구 numbers
- 갈아타요 linking
- 세워 주세요 rhythm

## 7. Input mission

- Follow a two-step route.
- Identify the correct exit/stop.

## 8. Output mission

- Ask one route question.
- Request a stop.

## 9. Reading

- Recognize simple station/exit signs.

## 10. Writing

- Write a route as two short steps.

## 11. Interaction mission

Ask, receive, confirm, and repair one wrong route on a map.

## 12. Exit task

You must be able to produce:

- 몇 번 출구예요?
- 여기 세워 주세요

You must be able to recognize:

- 갈아타요
- 반대 방향

**Success:** Reach the correct stop after one route correction.

## Production ceiling

One- or two-step routes only; no dense transit instructions.
