# Teacher Guide — Korean 1A Unit 06
# 교통에서 내릴 곳 말하기

Status: DRAFT_MATERIALIZED

## Can-do

- 교통수단에서 목적지·내릴 곳·출구를 짧게 묻고 확인할 수 있다.
- 에/에서/(으)로의 기본 공간 기능을 번역표가 아니라 장면으로 구별하기 시작한다.

## Communicative problem

길·출구·내릴 곳을 확인하고 틀린 방향이면 바로 repair한다.

## Relationship / register

- Primary relationship: traveler ↔ driver/staff/stranger
- Korean register: polite
- EN default: neutral polite
- DE default: Sie to staff/stranger

## Productive language

- 에: destination/location
- 에서: action/source contrast, limited use
- (으)로: direction/means, chunk-first
- -아/어 주세요: request stop/direction

## Recognition/context only

- 맞으시죠?: driver service-side confirmation; learner understands without productive analysis

## Teaching order

1. play/read the scene;
2. establish meaning;
3. notice core chunks;
4. explain only necessary form;
5. contrast native-use variation;
6. address EN/DE transfer risk;
7. run interaction task;
8. delayed retrieval.

## EN-L1 risk

- **EN-A1-E-ESEO**: Using one generic location marker because English 'at/in' does not align with Korean predicate-conditioned location marking.
- **EN-A1-PARTICLES-OMIT-REPLACE**: Particle omission or replacement remains a major error class for English-speaking Korean learners.

## DE-L1 risk

- **DE-A1-E-ESEO-EURO**: Mapping Korean location/direction particles directly onto German prepositions/cases.
- **DE-A1-PARTICLE-CASE**: Treating 은/는, 이/가, 을/를 as equivalents of German Nominativ/Akkusativ or articles.

## Pragmatics

- short route questions are natural
- app/UI wording differs from human speech

## Culture caution

- local transit shorthand is city-specific; teach Korean core plus optional city capsules.

Do not convert contextual patterns into universal rules.

## Pronunciation

- 출구 numbers
- 갈아타요 linking
- 세워 주세요 rhythm

## Assessment

Must produce:
- 몇 번 출구예요?
- 여기 세워 주세요

Must recognize:
- 갈아타요
- 반대 방향

Success:
reaches correct stop after one route correction

## Production ceiling

one- or two-step routes only; no dense transit instructions
