# Getting around and saying where to stop

## A. Meaning first

Match the Korean chunks to what they do in the situation.

- 여기 세워 주세요
- 몇 번 출구예요?
- 어디서 갈아타요?
- 이 버스 ___ 가요?

Do not translate word by word first. Identify the communicative job.
