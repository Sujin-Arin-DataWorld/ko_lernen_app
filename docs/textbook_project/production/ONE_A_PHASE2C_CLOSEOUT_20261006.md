# Phase 2C Closeout — Korean 1A Production Architecture

Date: 2026-10-06

## What Phase 2C solved

Phase 2B established the pedagogical content.

Phase 2C establishes **production constraints** so later units cannot drift in length, task density, review quality or app integration.

## Student book

Editorial target:
**144 pages**

Breakdown:
- front matter 4
- Hangul Zero 12
- 8 units × 14 = 112
- 4 pair reviews × 2 = 8
- final review 4
- appendix/index 4

## Workbook

Editorial target:
**96 pages**

Breakdown:
- front matter 4
- 8 units × 10 = 80
- pair reviews 8
- compact answer key 4

## Exercise density

Student unit:
- about 10 activity blocks
- closed-format max ~40%
- productive min ~35%
- interaction/retrieval min ~25%

Workbook:
- about 9 activity blocks
- 24–32 micro-items
- free production required
- delayed retrieval required
- EN/DE transfer diagnostic required

## Answer-key architecture

A structured answer-key schema now supports:
- exact answers;
- accepted variants;
- plausible-but-wrong responses;
- rationale;
- EN/DE feedback;
- partial-credit rubric;
- source/evidence references;
- transfer-risk references;
- human/pilot review status.

Unit 04 has a six-entry seed answer key.

## Learner pilot architecture

The pilot log records more than right/wrong:

- error type;
- transfer-risk ID;
- self-correction;
- prompt level;
- interaction breakdown;
- response time;
- confidence;
- immediate vs delayed retrieval.

Stages:
- baseline
- guided
- immediate post
- delayed 24h
- delayed 7d
- transfer

Pseudonymous participant codes are used; name/email are not required.

## Human review architecture

A single checklist now covers:
- Korean educator review;
- EN pedagogy;
- DE pedagogy;
- audio/spoken surface;
- visual/print density;
- assessment;
- rights/provenance;
- pilot readiness.

Model editorial completion does not equal human approval.

## Print ↔ app

29 verified content-ID links across all 8 units.

Core principle:
**print works offline; app enhances rather than completes the lesson.**

App routes are intentionally not invented.

## Validation checkpoint

Production validator checks:
- 144 / 96 page arithmetic;
- 16-page planning multiples;
- exercise-density limits;
- answer-key schema;
- pilot event schema;
- cross-link schema;
- cross-link IDs exist;
- all 8 units have dialogue/listening/recycling links;
- no fake deep links.

Current:
**PASS**
