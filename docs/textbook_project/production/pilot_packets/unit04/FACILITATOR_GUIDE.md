# Unit 04 Pilot Facilitator Guide

Purpose: discover whether the Unit 04 teaching design helps adult EN/DE learners independently order food/drink in Korean and retain the language over time.

## Important

- Do not coach toward a specific model sentence unless the stage allows prompting.
- Record what the learner actually does, including partial success.
- Do not show future-stage sheets in advance.
- Use pseudonymous participant codes only.
- If recording audio/video, obtain separate consent and follow a separate retention process.

## Stage order

1. Baseline — before teaching
2. Immediate post — after Unit 04
3. Delayed 24h — changed item/context
4. Delayed 7d — changed menu/context
5. Transfer — new service setting

## Prompt levels

0 — no help
1 — meaning/context cue
2 — first word / visual / choice cue
3 — model shown or repeated

## What to record

- result: correct / partial / incorrect / not_attempted
- error type
- transfer-risk ID when relevant
- self-correction
- prompt level
- interaction breakdown
- completion time
- learner confidence
- short teacher note

## Do not overcorrect during interaction

If Korean is understandable and the learner completes the communicative goal, let the turn finish first.
Correct after the interaction unless misunderstanding makes continuation impossible.

## Unit 04 target signals

Useful production includes:
- N 하나 주세요.
- N 한 잔 주세요.
- 많이 매워요?
- 안 매운 걸로 주세요.
- 포장해 주세요.

Do not require the learner to produce staff-side forms such as 주문하시겠어요? or 뭐 드릴까요?.

## EN transfer observation

Watch for:
- counter omission
- English SVO assembly
- translating 주세요 as 'give me' logic
- over-inserting particles after explicit instruction

## DE transfer observation

Watch for:
- numeral+noun transfer without counter
- overly literal German service-equivalent reasoning
- German word-order analogy overgeneralization
- treating particles like case/article marking

## Stage isolation rule

24h and 7d prompts must use changed lexical content so success is not simple sentence memory.

## End-of-pilot decision

After EN and DE cohorts, produce:
- top recurring errors
- high-value self-corrections
- explanation passages that caused confusion
- delayed-retention comparison
- KEEP / REWRITE / REORDER / MOVE_TO_RECOGNITION / PROMOTE_FORMULAIC recommendations.
