## Key correction to the audit model

A lower-level exercise may legitimately:
- recycle an easier word at a higher level;
- introduce a narrow situational survival word earlier than its general vocabulary-deck level;
- teach a fixed repair/service chunk before its full grammatical analysis;
- use a homographic surface form whose sense belongs to a different level.

Therefore lexical-string equality must never be treated as sense identity.
