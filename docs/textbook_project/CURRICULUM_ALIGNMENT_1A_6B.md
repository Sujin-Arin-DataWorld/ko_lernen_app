The publishing track uses 12 books while preserving the app's canonical six-level scale.

|---|---|---|---|---|

This is an editorial split, not a claim that CEFR, TOPIK, Sejong, Yonsei or NIKL levels are perfectly interchangeable.

## A/B split principle
A-book = establish the level's new communicative range with high-frequency situations and guided production.
B-book = deepen the same level with broader register, longer discourse, repair, mediation and transfer tasks.

## Hard inheritance
Each book inherits from `CONTENT_LEVEL_BIBLE.md`:
- grammar ceiling and level exceptions
- vocabulary ceiling / licensed official inventory
- sentence and clause limits
- speech-level expectations
- pronunciation targets
- culture scope
- DE/EN constraints
- prohibited difficulty patterns

## Modern publishing overlay
Book topics may be modernized, but grammar and lexical load may not be artificially raised just because a topic sounds contemporary.
A1 can discuss phones, delivery or messaging with A1 language; C1/C2 can discuss AI, housing, labor or media with evidence-aware advanced discourse.
