# Source Genre / Discourse Map — KO / EN / DE

Date: 2026-10-06

Purpose: record how **source genre changes language**. These are observed tendencies in sampled sources, not universal claims about all speakers.

The same topic must not be authored from one undifferentiated "native corpus". A news article, personal blog, book review, service interaction, and Reddit/community post solve different discourse problems and therefore use different grammar, stance, information order, and vocabulary.

## Korean source lanes

### Naver News / news-index lane

Observed sample traits:
- headline first, then source/actor + event + consequence;
- dense nouns and institutional labels;
- quotation marks and attributed speech are common;
- uncertainty is often carried by attribution rather than personal hedging;
- issues are framed as public consequence, policy, conflict, responsibility, or trend.

Useful for:
- B2–C2 news literacy;
- attribution verbs;
- stance/evidentiality;
- institutional vocabulary;
- headline vs body comparison.

Do not use as:
- model dialogue for A1/A2;
- evidence that ordinary people speak in news prose.

Sample:
- Naver News workplace issue index ("오늘도 출근", 2026)
- Naver News transport summaries mirrored by public index pages.

### Naver Blog — personal experience lane

Observed sample traits:
- first-person chronology: 먼저 → 그래서 → 전화해서/가서 → 결국;
- frequent evaluation: 생각보다, 괜찮았어요, 좀 당황했어요, 만족했어요;
- practical sequence + personal emotion are mixed;
- sentence endings lean heavily on -요, -더라고요, -것 같아요;
- emoji, ㅋㅋ/ㅎㅎ, repeated punctuation can mark stance rather than lexical content.

Useful for:
- A2–B2 real-life narrative;
- "what happened next" sequencing;
- customer-service, travel, interview, health-experience vocabulary;
- spoken-like connective rhythm.

Do not:
- vocalize ㅋㅋ/ㅎㅎ mechanically;
- treat one blogger's experience as a legal/medical rule.

### Korean review / 서평 lane

Observed sample traits:
- personal entry point → brief content summary → evaluation → recommendation/limitation;
- explicit stance markers: 마음에 들었다, 아쉬웠다, 추천하고 싶다, ~한 사람에게 맞다;
- "why" is often built from concrete features rather than abstract rating alone;
- reviews frequently separate "what the work does" from "what it did for me."

Useful for:
- B1 opinion;
- B2 evaluation;
- C1 critical review;
- recommendation language.

### Korean community / workplace-community lane

Observed sample traits:
- omitted subjects and compressed context;
- direct affective stance;
- fragments, rhetorical questions, slang, sarcasm;
- repair happens interactively in replies.

Useful for:
- spoken pragmatics;
- colloquial recognition;
- current community labels.

Risk:
- high volatility;
- community consensus != factual authority.

## English source lanes

### News

Observed sample traits:
- claim + source + number/evidence + counterpoint;
- explicit actor attribution;
- compact causal verbs: rose, fell, prompted, reflected, cited;
- quotes support or contest a claim rather than carrying the whole argument.

Useful for:
- B2–C2 source synthesis and evidence language.

### Blog / advice / career editorial

Observed sample traits:
- problem statement → actionable steps → model phrase;
- reader-direct "you";
- high use of examples and reusable chunks;
- professional but conversational tone.

Useful for:
- EN learner notes;
- workplace/interview formula banks.

### Goodreads / book-review lane

Observed sample traits:
- compact evaluation frames:
  - I liked that...
  - What worked for me...
  - I especially appreciated...
  - This didn't work for me...
- evaluation is frequently tied to readability, practicality, characters, structure, usefulness;
- subjective judgment is openly marked as personal.

Useful for:
- B1/B2 review writing;
- stance and justification.

### Reddit/community lane

Observed sample traits:
- fastest path to the point;
- ellipsis and fragments are normal;
- strong stance vocabulary and humor;
- local shorthand and assumed shared knowledge;
- replies supply correction, disagreement and collaborative repair.

Useful for:
- natural reactions;
- chat/casual register;
- discourse repair.

Risk:
- not factual authority for health, law or policy.

## German source lanes

### Tagesschau / news editorial

Observed sample traits:
- strong information hierarchy;
- institutional noun phrases;
- neutral attribution;
- causal/contrast linking without personal stance;
- titles often foreground public consequence.

Useful for:
- B2–C2 public-language and news literacy.

### StepStone / career editorial

Observed sample traits:
- direct reader address (du);
- checklist/problem-solution architecture;
- conventional employment vocabulary;
- clear separation of preparation, interview, follow-up.

Useful for:
- professional DE learner notes;
- job-interview domain vocabulary.

### Kununu / Trustpilot review lane

Observed sample traits:
- event chronology plus evaluation;
- highly reusable complaint frames:
  - keine Rückmeldung
  - sehr enttäuschend
  - ich warte noch auf...
  - Kundenservice antwortet nicht
- positive reviews use process adjectives:
  - unkompliziert
  - professionell
  - freundlich
  - transparent.

Useful for:
- B1–B2 consumer/workplace review;
- complaint vs praise contrast.

### LovelyBooks / book review lane

Observed sample traits:
- Inhalt / Gestaltung / Nutzbarkeit / Fazit style organization is common;
- evaluation with concessions:
  - hilfreich, aber...
  - gut strukturiert, allerdings...
  - für Einsteiger geeignet, für Fortgeschrittene eher...
- recommendation is audience-specific.

Useful for:
- B2 review writing;
- C1 nuanced evaluation.

### Reddit / r/arbeitsleben / city communities

Observed sample traits:
- colloquial contractions and Anglicisms;
- irony and hyperbole;
- direct complaint + concrete example;
- "mal ehrlich", "ich bin es leid", "keine Ahnung", "läuft" type stance framing;
- replies often challenge premises rather than merely answer.

Useful for:
- colloquial workplace/community comprehension;
- generational language.

# Cross-language genre lesson

A single semantic concept should be stored as:

**concept / function**
→ KO realization by genre
→ EN realization by genre
→ DE realization by genre

Example: "complaining about a delayed refund"

KO service:
- 환불금이 아직 안 들어왔어요. 확인해 주실 수 있을까요?

KO blog:
- 반품까지 했는데 환불이 생각보다 오래 걸렸어요.

EN service:
- I still haven't received my refund. Could you check the status?

EN review:
- I sent it back weeks ago and I'm still waiting for the refund.

DE service:
- Ich habe die Rückerstattung noch nicht erhalten. Können Sie den Status prüfen?

DE review:
- Ich warte seit Wochen auf die Rückerstattung.

The textbook should teach the **speech act and genre shift**, not a three-column translation table.

# Publishing implications

1. A1/A2 core dialogue uses service/casual lanes, not news prose.
2. B1 introduces personal blog/review narration.
3. B2 adds structured complaint, comparison and source distinction.
4. C1/C2 systematically compare news, review, community and institutional discourse.
5. Trend/slang from Reddit/community is recognition-first unless stable evidence supports production.
6. News and reviews are excellent material for reverse-direction KO→EN/DE learning because the same concept is expressed through different discourse architecture.

# Source-sampling rule going forward

For each priority topic, aim for:
- 1 institutional/authoritative or news source where relevant;
- 1 personal blog/experience source;
- 1 review or community source;
- additional source if one register lane is underrepresented.

For health/legal/safety:
- community = natural wording only;
- authoritative source = factual terminology and risk boundary.
