## Findings

### EN-C12-01 (required) — C1.2-02

Location: Shared materials alternative EN ‘the two approved photos’; modern script Maya EN ‘only the two approved photos’

KO counts people (‘동의된 두 사람의 사진’, ‘답한 두 사람 사진’), not photos. EN/DE turn that into exactly two approved photographs. Consent is person- and scope-specific in this task; the number of photographs is never given.

Proposed replacement: Alternative-card EN: ‘Either use only photos of the two people who agreed in an internal draft, or prepare text only. Neither option is agreed yet.’ Maya's first script row EN: ‘Posting today would be convenient. Shall we use only photos of the two people who have replied?’ Align DE ‘zwei freigegebenen Fotos’ to photos of the two people; retain internal-use-only limitation in the following reply.

Affected languages: EN, DE.

### EN-C12-03 (required) — C1.2-04

Location: Listening Maya row EN ‘I only heard the organiser’s plan, not the room’s readiness.’

KO contrasts hearing a plan with not personally seeing the preparation state. The English elliptical ‘heard … not … readiness’ changes the second clause into absence of a report, losing the firsthand-observation limit central to this lesson. DE has the same heard/not-heard structure.

Proposed replacement: Replace EN with: ‘I only heard the organiser’s plan to change rooms. I haven’t seen for myself whether the new room is ready.’ Align DE to ‘… den Vorbereitungsstand habe ich nicht selbst gesehen/geprüft’ rather than only not hearing it.

Affected languages: EN, DE.

### EN-C12-04 (required) — C1.2-05

The data names an unspecified resident as sender, but the model switches to first-person without assigning that resident's role to the learner. In an exercise explicitly about preserving agency, this role needs to be stated rather than silently changing the speaker into the sender.
