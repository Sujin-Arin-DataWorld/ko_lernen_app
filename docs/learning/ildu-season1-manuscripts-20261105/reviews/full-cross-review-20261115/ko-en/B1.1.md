## Required findings
### EN-B11-01
세 묶음 시범 EN ‘I was sorting photos and missed the call’; modern script turn1 ‘I missed your call while sorting the notices’; turn2 ‘Two sheets got wet.’

The translations weaken the target contrast: -느라고 explicitly gives the reason one could not answer, whereas ‘and/while’ can state only co-occurrence. The listening comprehension explicitly asks for distinct causes. The bare ‘got wet’ also omits the unwanted-outcome colouring of 젖고 말았어요.

Model: ‘I couldn’t answer the call because I was busy sorting photos.’ Modern turn1: ‘I couldn’t answer your call because I was busy sorting the notices. What happened?’ Modern turn2: ‘My bottle leaked inside the bag. Unfortunately, two sheets ended up getting wet.’
