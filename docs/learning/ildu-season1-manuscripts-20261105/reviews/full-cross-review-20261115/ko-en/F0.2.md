## EN-F02-01 — required — tree-and-hat, 생활 전이와 다음 장면

KO: “같은 표기가 두 뜻에 쓰이는 것을 설명과 함께 확인한다.” EN: “explain why both can be called 나무.” The English asks the F0 learner to produce an explanation, whereas Korean provides an explanation for supported confirmation. German has the same added demand. This changes the action and support level.

Replace the English transfer paragraph with:

> Find another tree or hat in a picture and add the Korean label. If you learnt the wood meaning, look at a tree photo and a photo of stacked boards side by side and review the explanation of the two meanings of 나무.

Keep KO. Coordinate DE with the German reviewer so it likewise asks the learner to review the provided explanation, not independently explain the semantic relation.
