## Mapping check

Our assumed 1↔A1, 2↔A2, 3↔B1, 4↔B2, 5↔C1, 6↔C2 mapping matches
`CONTENT_LEVEL_BIBLE.md` §A. The books' own self-labels only give a coarser
signal: levels 1 **and** 2 both say "초급" (beginner), levels 3 **and** 4
both say "중급" (intermediate) (`세종한국어 회화 익힘책_영어판_{1,2,3,4}-1.pdf`
p.6 each). This is directionally consistent but is not the books stating an
exact 1:1 grade — flagged, not silently confirmed. The vocab dictionary
(`1권_한국어 어휘사전(영어판).pdf` p.3) states its grading standard is the
same NIKL "국제 통용 한국어 표준 교육과정" (2017) our own
`nikl_kiiq_2017_*.csv` come from — real corroboration of our methodology,
but only a 초/중 (2-way) check; a full 5,039-headword cross-check was out of
scope this session (reported as a limitation, not attempted-and-hidden).

## 15 most consequential findings (all cited)

1. **입문 has zero extractable content anywhere in this folder** — the only
   file, `세종학당 한국어 입문(영어)_(low file size).pdf`, is 0% text
   (`inventory_2026-09-16.md`).
2. **B1 and B2 (Sejong 3, 4) have zero extractable content in the intended
   `세종학당 한국어` series** — all six related files (교재/익힘책/연습문제 ×
   2 levels) are 0% text; this report's B1/B2 numbers come from the
   substitute `회화` series only (see mapping-check above).
3. Honorific vocabulary **계시다/드리다/말씀** is taught by Sejong at A1
   (`세종한국어 회화 익힘책_영어판_1-2.pdf` unit 9, p.99) but sits at **B2** in
   `korean_vocab.csv` — a 7-level gap, and directly contradicts
   `CONTENT_LEVEL_BIBLE.md` §B.1⑥'s own claim that honorific basics
   (-으시-/께서) start at A1.
4. Core adjective pairs **가깝다/멀다** (A1 unit 11, p.99) and
   **밝다/어둡다** (same page) sit at A2/B1 in our vocab — these are basic,
   high-frequency A1 descriptive words per Sejong.
5. **지각하다/출근하다/퇴근하다/피곤하다** (A1 unit 14, p.100, the "reasons
   for absence" unit) sit at A2/B1 in ours, despite section B.1's own
   can-do list naming absence/lateness reasons as an A1 topic.
6. A1 grammar `-으시-/-시-` IS confirmed present in the extracted text
   (unit 10, p.94) — `CONTENT_LEVEL_BIBLE.md`'s claim that A1 introduces
   honorific `-으시-` is **confirmed**.
7. Of the bible's 45-item A1 grammar table, only **8/45** items were directly
   confirmed in the Sejong-1 grammar appendix's extracted text (see
   `comparison_2026-09-16.md`); the remaining 37 are "not found in extracted
   text of 세종한국어 회화 익힘책 1", not "not taught" — many (조사 은/을/이,
   선어말어미 -겠-/-었-) are basic morphology the appendix's quoted-label
   convention doesn't restate as a standalone entry, so this is a method
   ceiling, not evidence the bible is wrong.
8. `F4_sejong1_units.md`'s 14-unit table is **strongly corroborated**: 13/14
   titles match verbatim, and 24/28 listed grammar items are directly found
   at the cited unit; the 4 misses (`단위 명사(...)`, `안, -지 않다`,
   `-을까요?/-ㄹ까요?`, `한테, 한테(서)`) are compound labels our stricter
   quoted-label extraction only partially captures — spot-checked by hand,
   all 4 genuinely appear in the book text, just not via the automated
   anchor (`comparison_2026-09-16.md` F4 audit table).
9. A2 grammar `-기 때문에` and `-기로 하다` are placed at A2 in ours but

## Level-bible contradictions (cited)

- Section B.1's vocab-note claims honorific vocab is A1-appropriate;
  **계시다/드리다/말씀** are A1 in Sejong but B2 in our data (finding 3) —
  a real, cited contradiction of practice vs stated principle, not of the
  principle itself.
- No other direct section-B contradictions were found with certainty; most
  section-B claims for B1-C2 point to F1 (`F1_grammar_map.md`) rather than
  stating grammar inline, and this audit's B1/B2/C1/C2 grammar coverage
  from the extracted appendices is too partial (garbled/absent 문법 설명
  sections in several books, see inventory) to certify or refute those
  references confidently — reported as unverifiable, not confirmed.
