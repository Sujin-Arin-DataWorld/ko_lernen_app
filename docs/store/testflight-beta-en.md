## Beta App Description

Hangul Sori helps German- and English-speaking learners go from the Korean
alphabet to useful everyday Korean — inside a personal hanok (traditional
Korean home) that grows as you learn.

In this beta you can try:

• Hangul from scratch — consonants, vowels, and final consonants
• 558 vocabulary entries in themed packs, from A1 to B2
• 39 real-life scenarios (cafe, market, hotel, subway, pharmacy, and more)
• Grammar in context, plus listening, translation, and review
• Special quests with Bojagi rewards that furnish rooms in your hanok
• Mini-games: initial-consonant quiz, Hangul Wordle, and Kkeunmari (Korean word-chain)
• Optional Snap-and-Learn: photograph or pick a textbook page and work with the Korean text on your device

Your learning progress stays on your device unless you deliberately link a
Google or Apple account for cloud backup. There are no ads and no cross-app
tracking.

We'd love your feedback — email hello@hangul-sori.com or use the in-app
feedback button after a lesson.

## What to Test — build 2.0.5

Thanks for testing Hangul Sori 2.0.5! Please focus on:

1. Onboarding — pick a starting level (A1–B2) and reach the home screen.
2. Hanok map — check that it grows, and that today's recommended study opens from the Sarangbang.
3. Vocabulary packs — learn → quiz → boss round; confirm the next pack unlocks after ~70%.
4. Quests & Bojagi rewards — complete a quest and furnish a room.
5. Snap-and-Learn — camera/photo permission → capture → crop → OCR → analysis result.
6. Mini-games — initial-consonant quiz, Hangul Wordle, Kkeunmari.
7. iPad / tablet layout — spacing and readability on larger screens.
8. Dark mode — readability across every screen.

Please report anything that crashes, looks cut off, or reads incorrectly —
by email (hello@hangul-sori.com) or the in-app feedback button.
