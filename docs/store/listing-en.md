## Description

Hangul Sori is a Korean-learning app for German and English speakers who want
to move from the alphabet to useful everyday Korean.

Learn inside a personal hanok: your traditional Korean home is also your map.
Its spaces grow with your learning, so progress feels like building a place
rather than ticking off an endless lesson list. In the Sarangbang, the app
opens the same recommended next study that your learning plan chose; it does
not create a separate lesson stream. Complete special quests, open Bojagi
rewards, and use the decorations you collect to furnish rooms in your hanok.

WHAT YOU CAN LEARN

• Hangul from the ground up: consonants, vowels, and final consonants.
• 558 vocabulary entries in 64 themed packs, from A1 to B2.
• 39 real-life scenarios for places such as a cafe, market, hotel, subway, and
  pharmacy.
• Grammar in context, listening, translation, and review activities.
• 17 special quests with goals and rewards.
• Mini-games including an initial-consonant quiz, Hangul Wordle, and
  Kkeunmari, the Korean word-chain game.
• Optional Snap-and-Learn: photograph or select a textbook page to work with
  Korean text on your device.

WHY HANGUL SORI FEELS DIFFERENT

• A personal hanok map gives your study a visible home.
• The Sarangbang gives today's recommended study a meaningful place to begin.
• Bojagi rewards let completed quests become room decoration choices.
• Korean folk-painting-inspired visuals, a tiger, and a magpie make the app
  feel like a place to return to.
• A responsive layout is designed for phones and tablets.

Choose a starting level from A1 to B2 and keep learning Korean, one useful
step at a time.
